# Finance Circular 14/2026

Issued by the Office of the Registrar.

## §1 Applicability
This circular amends the Finance Policy with immediate effect and applies to all pending and future requests.

## §3 Revised Procurement Thresholds
§3.1 Effective immediately, purchases exceeding Rs 50,000 shall additionally require approval from the Registrar.

§3.2 The approval of the Registrar under §3.1 is sought after the approval of the Dean of Student Affairs.

§3.3 All other thresholds stated in the Finance Policy remain unchanged.
