# Security Guidelines

## §1 Scope
These guidelines govern the security clearance of events, visitors and gatherings on campus.

## §4 External Speakers and Guests
§4.1 Events featuring external speakers or guests require clearance from the Security Office before the event date.

§4.2 Identity details of every external guest must be recorded and retained by the organising club for a period of one year.

§4.3 The Security Office may require a list of external guests no later than forty-eight hours before the event.

## §5 Large Gatherings
§5.1 Events exceeding 500 attendees require additional security deployment, arranged by the Security Office at the organiser's cost.

§5.2 Events held after 21:00 require prior intimation to the Security Office.
