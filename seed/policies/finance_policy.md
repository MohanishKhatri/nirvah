# Finance Policy

## §1 Scope
This policy governs expenditure, reimbursement and procurement across all departments and student bodies.

## §5 Routing of Expenditure
§5.1 All requests involving expenditure of institutional funds must route through the Finance Office for verification of budget availability.

§5.2 The Finance Office shall verify the availability of funds before any approval is granted by higher authority.

## §8 Expenditure Approval Thresholds
§8.1 Expenditure not exceeding Rs 25,000 may be approved at the departmental level.

§8.2 Student activity expenditure exceeding Rs 25,000 requires approval from the Dean of Student Affairs.

§8.3 Procurement of goods or equipment exceeding Rs 25,000 requires approval from the Dean of Student Affairs.

## §9 Procurement Documentation
§9.1 Three vendor quotations are required for any purchase above Rs 50,000.

§9.2 Quotations must be dated within sixty days of the request.

## §10 Reimbursement
§10.1 Reimbursement claims must be filed within thirty days of expenditure, supported by original invoices.
