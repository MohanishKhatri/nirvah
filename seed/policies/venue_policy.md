# Venue Policy

## §1 Scope
This policy governs the booking and use of institutional venues, halls and open spaces.

## §2 Venue Approval
§2.1 All venue bookings are administered by the Venue Office.

§2.2 Bookings are granted on a first-come basis subject to academic priority.

§2.3 Events with more than 100 attendees require approval from the Venue Office.

§2.4 Seminar Hall 2 has a maximum capacity of 200 persons. Requests exceeding this capacity shall not be granted.

§2.5 The Main Auditorium has a maximum capacity of 800 persons.

## §3 Booking Timelines
§3.1 Venue bookings must be made at least seven days in advance of the event date.

§3.2 Cancellations must be intimated at least forty-eight hours in advance.
