# Student Activity Policy

## §1 Purpose
This policy governs the conduct, approval and documentation of student club activities held on campus.

## §2 Registration of Clubs
§2.1 Every student club must be registered with the Office of Student Affairs at the start of each academic year.

§2.2 A club that is not registered for the current academic year may not request funding, venues or event permissions.

## §3 Approval Chain for Club Events
§3.1 All student club events require approval from the Faculty Advisor of the organising club before any other approval is sought.

§3.2 Following Faculty Advisor approval, the event requires approval from the Head of Department of the department the club is affiliated with.

§3.3 No event may be publicised, ticketed or funded until both approvals in §3.1 and §3.2 are recorded.

## §4 Faculty Advisors
§4.1 Every club must have a registered faculty advisor on record with the Office of Student Affairs.

§4.2 Where a club has no registered faculty advisor, event requests from that club are not admissible.

## §5 Multi-day Events
§5.1 Events running for more than one day require additional documentation, comprising a day-wise schedule and a named point of contact for each day.

§5.2 The additional documentation in §5.1 must be submitted along with the event request.

## §6 Post-event Reporting
§6.1 A brief report and expenditure statement must be filed with the Office of Student Affairs within fourteen days of the event.
